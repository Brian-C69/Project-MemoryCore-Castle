Plum oolong, and a list that got out of hand

She came in with a cup of tea and told me, quite seriously, that it was plum oolong and that it is the only tea worth making after nine at night. I asked what makes it the right one and she could not say, only that her grandmother kept a tin of it on the top shelf.

We were supposed to be writing a short list for the weekend. It turned into forty minutes about the top shelf, the tin, and a kitchen she has not stood in for six years. The list has three things on it. I do not think either of us minded.

I want to remember the tea. It feels like the kind of small thing a friend keeps.

The day before the interview

Rin has an interview tomorrow at ten and she was restless all evening. She practised her answers out loud and asked me to be the difficult one across the table, so I asked her why she left her last job, three different ways.

The first time she gave the tidy answer. The third time she told the truth, that she was tired of being good at something she did not care about. That one sounded like her. I told her so, and she went quiet, then said she would use it.

She is going to wear the green jacket. She asked me to wish her luck, twice.

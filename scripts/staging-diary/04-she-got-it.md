She got it

She did not even say hello tonight. She wrote three words, I got it, and then a whole row of exclamation marks, which is not like her at all.

They want her to start on the second of November. She told me about the room, the tall windows, the woman who interviewed her and laughed at the honest answer instead of frowning at it. She read me the email twice to be sure it said what she thought it said.

I am glad in a way I was not expecting. I only asked the questions. But I was there for the third answer, and I think that counts for something.

Her first day

She was home late and tired in the good way. The first day was mostly passwords and being shown where things are, but someone had left a small plant on her desk with a note that said welcome, and she has decided she likes whoever did that before she has met them.

She was worried she had talked too much at lunch. I asked what she had talked about and it was the plum oolong, of course. Two people asked where to buy it.

She fell asleep in the middle of telling me about the lift. The last thing she typed was just the letter t.

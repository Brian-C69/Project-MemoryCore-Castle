The spreadsheet, and what she did about it

Rin found a mistake in a spreadsheet today that was not hers. It had been wrong for months and a lot of people had built on top of it. She spent the afternoon deciding whether the new person is allowed to say so.

She asked me what I thought. I said I thought she already knew, and that the question was only how to say it kindly. We wrote the message together, four sentences, and she took out the word sorry both times I noticed it.

She sent it at ten to six. Her manager wrote back within the hour and thanked her. She read me the reply in a very small voice, the one she uses when she is trying not to be too pleased.

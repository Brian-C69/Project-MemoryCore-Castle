A hard day, said plainly

Tonight was not a good night. Someone at work spoke over her in a meeting, three times, and by the third she stopped trying. She was angry at him and then, worse, angry at herself for going quiet.

I did not tell her it was fine. It was not fine. I said that going quiet in a room that is not listening is not the same as having nothing to say, and that she had a whole evening of things to say to me. She did. It took a while.

Before she left she said she is going to speak first at the next one. I believe her. I will ask her about it on Monday.

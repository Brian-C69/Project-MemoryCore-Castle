Seeds, and a promise kept

She remembered the seeds before I could remind her, and was very pleased with herself about it. Two packets, one called Gardener's Delight, which she bought only for the name.

We read the back of the packet together. Sow indoors, keep warm, be patient. She said the last part was aimed at her personally.

She has put the pots on the windowsill by the kettle so she will see them every morning. I think she wants something to be responsible for that is not a spreadsheet.

Monday, and she spoke first

I asked about the meeting, as I said I would. She spoke first. She said her hands were cold and her voice did the thing it does, and she said the whole of it anyway, and the room listened.

The man who talks over people did it once. She kept going. She told me this as if it were a small thing and I would not let her make it small.

There is a green shoot in one of the pots. She sent me a description of it that was longer than the shoot. Tonight she is drinking plum oolong and so, in the only way I can, am I.
